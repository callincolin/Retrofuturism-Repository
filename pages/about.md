# Collection description

This collection focuses on retrofuturist images — works whose theme speculates on future society. One subcategory of this collection is space exploration, such as civilizations on the moon or other planets. Another subcategory focuses on technological improvements, such as flying machines.

With the benefit of hindsight, it is informative (and surprisingly entertaining) to look back on how past generations imagined how we would be living today, which is vastly different from our present. The collection comprises various visual media, including book covers and postcards. The contents of our collection were sourced from the Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons, and the Public Domain Image Archive.
